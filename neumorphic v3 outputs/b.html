Common Questions

Do I need a gym membership? +
No. Our programming is designed to be adaptable. We provide gym variations, hotel-room HIIT options, and home-bodyweight routines.
What makes 45 minutes enough? +
Efficiency. By removing rest periods between exercises and utilizing supersets, we maintain high heart rate output without burning you out physically. Science shows 45 mins is the sweet spot for cortisol management.
Can I work out anytime? +
Yes. Since everything is digital and self-paced, you can train at 5 AM or 9 PM depending on your energy levels.
