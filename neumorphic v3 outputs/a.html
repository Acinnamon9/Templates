Is AlphaAI registered with any financial regulators? +

Yes! We're a Registered Investment Adviser (RIA) with the SEC and carry SIPC insurance up to $500K per client. All operations comply with FINRA regulations.

Can I transfer money in and out anytime? +

Absolutely. Withdrawals typically process within 1-3 business days via ACH transfer to your linked bank account. No penalties, no locks.

What happens if the market crashes? +

Our AI detects volatility patterns and automatically adjusts allocations toward safer assets. Historically, AlphaAI portfolios recover 30% faster than passive index funds during downturns [[1]].

How secure are my bank details? +

We use military-grade 256-bit AES encryption and never store raw banking credentials. All connections are through verified, read-only APIs from major banks using Plaid integration.

What's the difference between AI and human advisors? +

AI processes data 24/7 without emotion, adjusting in milliseconds to market shifts—a human advisor would take hours or days to react to the same changes [[2]]. Plus lower fees.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes, no contracts or cancellation fees. Cancel through settings—we'll even help move your holdings wherever you prefer. Money-back guarantee for first 30 days.
