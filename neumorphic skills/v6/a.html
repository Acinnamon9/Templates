Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Is my content kept private?

Absolutely. Your content never leaves your secure environment for model training. All processing happens with enterprise-grade encryption, and we don't use your data to train any public models. Read our detailed privacy policy for full transparency.

Which platforms does Brevity integrate with?

We currently offer native integrations with Google Docs, Notion, and Slack. API access is available for Enterprise plans if you need custom workflows. More integrations are coming soon—including WordPress and Medium.

Can I choose how aggressive the suggestions are?

Yes! Every plan includes three intensity levels: Light (conservative editing), Medium (balanced optimization), and Aggressive (maximum reduction). You can also customize thresholds based on document type or audience.

What's the learning curve for new users?

Most users are fully productive within 15 minutes. Brevity feels familiar—you work inside your existing tools. The interface shows clear explanations for each suggestion, so you learn as you go. Onboarding calls included for Growth and Enterprise plans.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts, no cancellation fees, and no lock-ins. Your team retains access until your billing period ends. If you cancel mid-cycle, you can re-enable anytime without losing data or preferences.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

We absolutely love supporting missions that matter. Eligible nonprofit organizations and accredited educational institutions receive 50% off all plans. Reach out to our team with verification details and we'll get you set up.
