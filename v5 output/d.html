Common Objections

Do I need a gym membership?

+

Ideally yes, but not mandatory. About 40% of my clients are "Home Gym" optimized using adjustable dumbbells and kettlebells. We design around the barbell, but we respect the reality of limited space.

I travel 2 weeks a month. Will this work?

+

Absolutely. That's actually a specialty of mine. We build hotel-room friendly routines and provide travel nutrition guides so you can maintain mass on the road.

What happens if I miss a week?

+

Life happens. Life continues. Just message me, tell me when you're back, and we adjust your next cycle to prioritize that window.
