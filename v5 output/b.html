Common Questions

Is my data safe?

+

Absolutely. Brevity is SOC2 Type II compliant. We do not train on your private data, and enterprise plans support VPC deployment for total isolation.

Does it write from scratch?

+

No, Brevity is a refinement engine. It requires source material (drafts, outlines, raw notes) to compress and polish. You still need the ideas; we handle the editing.

Can I cancel anytime?

+

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. Cancel your subscription directly from the dashboard, and you keep access until the end of your billing cycle.

Does it support other languages?

+

Currently we support English, Spanish, French, and German, with full localization plans coming in Q3.

What about the free trial?

+

We offer a 7-day free trial on all paid plans so you can test the Team features before committing.
