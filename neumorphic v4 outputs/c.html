Questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know about getting started with Brevity.

Does Brevity work offline?

+
Brevity requires an internet connection to process text through our AI models. However, the extension UI loads instantly and your drafts are never stored on our servers after processing.

Will it change my brand voice?

+
Never. Brevity's Tone Lock™ technology learns your existing writing style and preserves it while removing fluff. You can also upload a custom style guide for team-wide consistency.

Is my content secure?

+
Absolutely. Brevity is SOC 2 Type II compliant. Your text is encrypted in transit and at rest, never used to train public models, and automatically deleted after 24 hours unless you opt into anonymized improvement programs.

Can I try before I buy?

+
Yes! The Starter plan is free forever with 50 Brevifies per month. Team plan users get a full 14-day free trial with unlimited access to all features—no credit card required to start.

What if I need help?

+
Our support team responds within 2 hours during business hours. Team and Enterprise plans include priority support, dedicated onboarding, and access to our community of power users.
