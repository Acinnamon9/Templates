Common Questions

Do I need a gym membership?

Not necessarily. While having full access helps, our members have success training in hotel gyms, parks, or with dumbbells at home. The app allows you to swap exercises dynamically.

How often do I talk to my coach?

In Group Coaching, we have bi-weekly calls. In Elite, we chat directly whenever you hit a snag or need a modification. We prioritize clarity and speed over bureaucracy.

Is there a commitment length?

Nope. We operate on a month-to-month basis because results compound over time. As long as you're making progress, stay. If you need a break, pause whenever.
