Common Inquiries

Do you work with international banks? +
Yes. We have established relationships with major international lending institutions that specialize in non-resident mortgages, allowing for up to 70% LTV on premium assets.
What are the costs associated with buying as a foreign national? +
Aside from standard closing costs (~2% of purchase price), foreign buyers should budget for FIRPTA withholding taxes and potential property tax adjustments based on residency status. Our team provides a full pro-forma before contract signing.
Can I tour properties without flying to Miami immediately? +
Absolutely. For serious buyers, we offer full 3D Matterport virtual tours and FaceTime walkthroughs led by our listing agents to narrow down options before travel.
