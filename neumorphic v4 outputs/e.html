Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with international buyers?

+

Absolutely. Over 60% of our clients are international. We specialize in navigating FIRPTA regulations, setting up US LLCs for privacy, and coordinating remote closings via Power of Attorney.

What is the average price point of your listings?

+

We focus exclusively on the luxury segment, with an average sale price of $4.2M. Our portfolio ranges from $2M condos in Brickell to $50M+ waterfront estates in Star Island and Indian Creek.

Can you help with property management?

+

Yes. We offer full-service concierge management including rental administration, maintenance coordination, and interior design referrals to ensure your asset maintains its value.

How do I schedule a private showing?

+

Simply use the contact form below or call our direct line. We arrange private helicopter tours, yacht viewings, and exclusive access to properties not yet on the MLS.
