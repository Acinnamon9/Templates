Below is a **practical `copy_patterns.md`** intended for AI systems that generate **high-conversion landing pages**.

The goal is to give the AI **copywriting structures**, not just stylistic advice, so it can consistently produce persuasive text rather than vague SaaS filler.

---

# copy_patterns.md

## Purpose

This document defines **conversion-oriented copywriting frameworks** used when generating landing page content.

AI must follow these frameworks when writing:

* headlines
* subheadlines
* feature descriptions
* CTAs
* testimonials
* benefit statements

The goal is **clarity, persuasion, and specificity**.

---

# Global Copy Principles

### Clarity Over Cleverness

Copy must prioritize **immediate comprehension**.

Avoid:

```text
Reinventing the future of innovation
Unlocking limitless possibilities
Transforming the digital experience
```

Prefer:

```text
Book more sales calls from your website
Generate qualified leads automatically
Launch high-converting landing pages in minutes
```

---

### Specificity Beats Abstraction

Prefer **numbers, timeframes, and outcomes**.

Weak:

```text
Improve your marketing performance
```

Strong:

```text
Generate 3–5 qualified leads per day from your landing page
```

---

### Speak to One Audience

Each page must assume **a single primary audience**.

Examples:

```text
SaaS founders
Local service businesses
Marketing agencies
Course creators
```

Avoid speaking to **multiple audiences simultaneously**.

---

# Hero Copy Frameworks

The hero section contains the **most important copy on the page**.

It must communicate:

* who the product is for
* what outcome it delivers
* how quickly or easily

---

# Hero Headline Pattern

Primary formula:

```text
Result + timeframe + audience
```

Examples:

```text
Get qualified leads in 14 days without paid ads
Launch a high-converting landing page in under an hour
Automate your agency client onboarding in one workflow
```

---

Alternative formula:

```text
Desired outcome + mechanism
```

Examples:

```text
Turn your website into a 24/7 sales funnel
Build landing pages that convert visitors into booked calls
```

---

# Hero Subheadline Pattern

The subheadline explains:

```text
Mechanism + credibility
```

Examples:

```text
A lightweight funnel builder used by 1,200+ agencies to generate leads without complex tools.
```

```text
Our framework combines proven conversion psychology with fast deployment so your page can start capturing leads immediately.
```

---

# Hero CTA Copy

CTA buttons should contain **clear actions**.

Preferred structure:

```text
Verb + outcome
```

Examples:

```text
Book a Demo
Start Free Trial
Get the Template
Launch My Funnel
```

Avoid vague CTAs:

```text
Submit
Click Here
Continue
Learn More
```

---

# Problem Section Copy

Purpose: **make the reader feel understood**.

Structure:

```text
Problem headline
Problem explanation
Bullet list of frustrations
```

---

## Problem Headline Pattern

```text
Why most [activity] fails
```

Example:

```text
Why most landing pages never generate leads
```

---

## Problem Bullet Pattern

Each bullet should contain:

```text
problem + consequence
```

Example:

```text
Your website gets traffic but no one converts
Your funnel tools are too complex to maintain
Visitors leave before understanding your offer
```

---

# Solution Section Copy

Purpose: introduce the **mechanism that solves the problem**.

Structure:

```text
headline
short explanation
feature blocks
```

---

## Solution Headline Pattern

```text
Meet the system that fixes this
```

or

```text
A simpler way to [desired outcome]
```

Examples:

```text
A simpler way to launch high-converting landing pages
```

---

# Feature Copy Pattern

Each feature must follow:

```text
feature name
short benefit explanation
```

Example:

**Instant Page Builder**

```text
Create full landing pages in minutes without coding.
```

---

Avoid describing features **without benefits**.

Weak:

```text
Drag and drop editor
```

Strong:

```text
Drag-and-drop editor that lets you launch pages in minutes.
```

---

# Benefits Section Copy

Purpose: translate **features into outcomes**.

Pattern:

```text
benefit title
supporting explanation
```

Example:

**Launch Faster**

```text
Deploy complete landing pages in under an hour.
```

---

**Capture More Leads**

```text
Conversion-focused layouts turn visitors into booked calls.
```

---

# Social Proof Copy

Purpose: build trust.

Allowed formats:

```text
testimonials
metrics
client logos
case studies
```

---

# Testimonial Structure

```text
quote
name
role
company
```

Example:

```text
"This landing page increased our demo bookings by 40%."

— Sarah Kim  
Head of Growth, FinTechly
```

---

# Metrics Copy

Numbers should be **large and easily scannable**.

Examples:

```text
10,000+ users
4.8 average rating
500+ companies using the platform
```

---

# Offer Section Copy

Purpose: present the product or service clearly.

Structure:

```text
headline
offer explanation
pricing or plan descriptions
CTA
```

---

## Offer Headline Pattern

```text
Start building your funnel today
```

or

```text
Everything you need to launch high-converting pages
```

---

# Pricing Card Copy

Each pricing card must contain:

```text
plan name
price
feature list
CTA
```

Feature list rules:

* short
* outcome-oriented
* max 6 items

Example:

```text
Unlimited landing pages
Built-in analytics
Conversion templates
CRM integrations
```

---

# FAQ Copy

Purpose: remove objections.

Questions should address:

```text
price concerns
implementation effort
technical requirements
results expectations
```

Example questions:

```text
How long does it take to launch a page?
Do I need coding skills?
Can I connect my CRM?
```

---

# CTA Section Copy

Purpose: trigger final conversion.

Structure:

```text
headline
short reinforcement statement
primary CTA
```

Example:

Headline:

```text
Start generating leads today
```

Support:

```text
Launch your first funnel in minutes.
```

CTA:

```text
Start Free Trial
```

---

# CTA Verb Library

Allowed verbs:

```text
Start
Get
Launch
Book
Join
Create
Build
Claim
Access
Download
```

Avoid weak verbs:

```text
Submit
Proceed
Continue
```

---

# Tone Guidelines

The tone should be:

```text
clear
direct
confident
helpful
```

Avoid:

```text
corporate jargon
hype language
buzzwords
```

Example of **bad tone**:

```text
Revolutionizing the paradigm of digital synergy
```

Example of **good tone**:

```text
Build landing pages that convert visitors into leads.
```

---

# Sentence Length

Guidelines:

```text
headline: 6–12 words
subheadline: 12–24 words
paragraph: 1–3 sentences
feature explanation: 10–20 words
```

Short sentences improve **scanability**.

---

# Paragraph Rules

Avoid large blocks of text.

Maximum paragraph length:

```text
3 sentences
```

Prefer:

```text
short paragraphs
bullets
visual hierarchy
```

---

# Forbidden Copy Patterns

Do not generate:

```text
buzzword-heavy marketing copy
generic SaaS promises
empty inspirational statements
vague benefits
```

Examples of forbidden phrases:

```text
next-generation platform
game-changing solution
innovative ecosystem
cutting-edge technology
```

---

# Assembly Logic for AI

When generating copy for a page:

```text
1 identify the target audience
2 determine the desired outcome
3 construct hero headline using headline formula
4 explain mechanism in subheadline
5 present features
6 translate features into benefits
7 add social proof
8 present offer
9 end with strong CTA
```
