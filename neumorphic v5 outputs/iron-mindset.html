Questions? Answers.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do I need gym equipment?

+
No. Our programs are designed for home, hotel, or garage gym training. Minimal equipment — dumbbells and a pull-up bar cover 90% of movements. We also offer bodyweight-only tracks.

Is 45 minutes really enough?

+
Absolutely. Research shows that high-intensity, well-programmed sessions of 40-50 minutes produce superior results to longer, unfocused workouts. We maximize every minute.

What if I'm a complete beginner?

+
We welcome all levels. Your initial assessment determines your starting point, and every exercise has scaled variations. Your protocol is built for where you are today, not where someone else is.

Can I cancel anytime?

+
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. We keep 94% of our members because the results speak for themselves — not because you're locked in.

What time zones do you support?

+
We have coaches across US, EU, and APAC time zones. You'll always find a live session that fits your schedule, whether you're an early bird or a night owl.

How is this different from Peloton or a gym membership?

+
Peloton is content. A gym is a building. Iron Mindset is a coach who knows your name, your numbers, and your goals — and adjusts your program every single week. It's personalized, not programmatic.
